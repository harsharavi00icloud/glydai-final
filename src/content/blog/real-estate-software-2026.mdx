## Transaction Management: Keep Deals from Falling Apart

These tools manage contracts, e-signatures, compliance checklists and deadlines in one place.

- **Best tool — Dotloop:** Deeply integrated with brokerages and easy for clients to use ($31/mo)
- **Affordable alternative — DocuSign** for e-signatures only (free tier available)

Without these tools, you'll be chasing PDFs over emails and hoping nothing slips through.

Most realtors use a combination of a CRM, a portal like Zillow or Realtor.com for leads and others. The gap in most agents is automated follow-up — which is where deals are lost.
