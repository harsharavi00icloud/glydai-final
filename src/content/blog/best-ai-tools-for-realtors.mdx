## Real Estate Content AI

**Saleswise** has 40+ specialized tools including live market data, virtual staging, listing descriptions, CMAs and email campaigns. These tools can help you 10x the way you work because you can pick and choose specialized ones for every need. But 40+ tools can get you overwhelmed, especially if these tools don't integrate with your existing workflows.

**Write.homes** helps realtors generate MLS listing copy, social media posts and email copy. It's a great tool to save time and it includes tailored templates, multilingual support and a free trial. Independent reviews are sparse — it's mostly self-promoted.

**REimagineHome** is an AI tool for virtual staging, interior design, decluttering and landscaping for real room photos. It's fast, generates photorealistic results, easy to use and extremely cost effective versus traditional staging. Although, it limits free generation and has restricted customization.

> **Verdict:** Out of the three, Saleswise saves you the most time and REimagineHome produces the best output. But AI content still needs human editing. These tools are just like a junior marketing assistant — great at execution, useless at strategy.

## The 3 Mistakes Real Estate Agents Make With AI Tools

**1. Using AI for low-leverage tasks**

Remember the "87% brokerages use AI" statistic? This is what most of the 87% of brokerages do. They use AI to generate Instagram post captions. This might save you a couple of minutes but using AI for higher leverage tasks like lead qualification and follow-up will save you hours per week and bring higher quality leads.

**2. Buying AI tools separately instead of integrating them**

Multiple tools, multiple logins and more clutter. This is not how to use AI as a real estate agent. You're already juggling multiple micro tasks every day. Instead, you need AI tools that seamlessly integrate with tools you already use and complement your style of working. Not an AI that adds more clutter.

**3. Expecting AI to replace relationships**

It can't — but it CAN make you available 24/7 for the first touchpoint. The first agent to respond wins 78% of the time (MIT Study). AI is like hiring a great receptionist; not replacing yourself.
