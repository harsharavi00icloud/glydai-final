## What A CRM Actually Does (And What It Doesn't)

A CRM (Contact Relationship Manager) is a software where you keep everything you know about your contacts - who they are, what they bought, what they like, how to contact them. It's core job is to ensure no lead or client, past or present ever falls through a crack.

## What To Look For In A Real Estate CRM (And What To Ignore)

Here are the must-haves for every agent in 2026:

- Lead Capture from Zillow & Facebook - You're spending money on ads or Zillow Leads and it's important you don't waste them
- Automated email + text drip sequences
- MLS/IDX integration
- Mobile App
- Pipeline / Transaction stages view
- API or Zapier access

Here's what you can ignore: Features that look good but rarely matter like built-in social media posting, news feeds, mortgage calculator etc.

If your CRM doesn't have API or Zapier access, it cannot be 10x'd with AI tools. And that's a deal-breaker in 2026.
